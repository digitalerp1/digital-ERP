1. Introduction

Welcome to My Zini ERP ("we," "our," or "us"). We provide a SaaS platform that enables businesses to manage multiple social media accounts, automate WhatsApp messaging, and handle ERP functions via a unified dashboard.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website https://digitalerp.shop or use our software. We act as a Tech Provider facilitating your connection to services provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp).

By using our Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal & Business Information

To create your account and bill you for services, we collect:

  • Identity Data: Name, Email Address, Phone Number.
  • Business Data: Company Name, Tax ID (GST/VAT), Address.
  • Authentication Data: Encrypted passwords and OAuth tokens (for Facebook/Google login).

2.2 Platform Data (Meta Services)

When you connect your Facebook, Instagram, or WhatsApp accounts, we collect specific data to enable our features:

  • Page/Account IDs: Unique identifiers for your connected pages and accounts.
  • Access Tokens: System User Tokens required to perform actions on your behalf (e.g., sending a message).
  • Content Data: The text, images, and media contained in posts, comments, and messages required for the dashboard display.
  • Analytics Data: Impressions, clicks, and engagement metrics to show you performance reports.

3. Meta API Permissions & Usage Justification

My Zini ERP requires specific permissions ("scopes") from Meta to function. We only request access necessary to provide the features you utilize. Below is a detailed breakdown of these permissions:

WhatsApp Business API

  • whatsapp_business_messaging: Required to send automated replies, marketing campaigns, and transactional messages on your behalf.
  • whatsapp_business_management: Used to register your phone number, manage WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) settings, and sync message templates.

Instagram Graph API

  • instagram_basic: To fetch your profile info (name, handle) to display in the dashboard.
  • instagram_content_publish: Allows you to schedule and publish photos/videos to Instagram from our ERP.
  • instagram_manage_messages: Essential for our "Unified Inbox" feature, enabling you to read and reply to DMs.
  • instagram_manage_comments: To track comments on your posts and allow you to reply or hide offensive comments.
  • instagram_manage_insights: To fetch and display performance analytics (likes, reach, engagement).

Facebook Page Management

  • pages_show_list: To show you a dropdown of Pages you manage so you can select which one to connect.
  • pages_read_engagement: To read content posted to your Page for analytics and dashboard display.
  • pages_manage_posts: To publish posts created in our ERP scheduler to your Facebook Page.
  • pages_messaging: To enable two-way communication with your customers via Facebook Messenger within our tool.
  • pages_manage_metadata: To subscribe to Webhooks, ensuring you receive real-time notifications for new messages/leads.

4. How We Use Your Data

We process your data for the following specific business purposes:

  1. Service Functionality: To execute the commands you issue via the dashboard (e.g., sending a WhatsApp broadcast, replying to an Instagram DM).
  2. Automation: To trigger automated workflows (Chatbots) based on rules you define.
  3. Performance Analytics: To aggregate data from your connected accounts and present it in visual charts.
  4. Security & Verification: To verify your identity and prevent unauthorized access to your business accounts.
  5. Communication: To send critical alerts regarding your account status, API connection expiry, or subscription billing.

5. Technical Infrastructure & Security

We utilize industry-leading technologies to ensure your data is secure, available, and private.

Vercel (Hosting)

Our application is hosted on Vercel, ensuring high availability, secure HTTPS (TLS 1.3) connections, and strict access controls for deployment.

Supabase (Database)

Data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL). We implement Row Level Security (RLS) so users can strictly access only their own data. Data is encrypted at rest (AES-256).

Cloudflare (Network)

We use Cloudflare for DNS management, DDoS protection, and Web Application Firewall (WAF) to block malicious traffic before it reaches our servers.

6. Data Sharing & Disclosure

Strict No-Sale Policy: We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others.

We may share generic aggregated demographic information not linked to any personal identification information regarding visitors and users with our business partners for analytical purposes.

Transfers to Meta Platforms

By using our WhatsApp/Facebook/Instagram integration, you acknowledge that content (messages, media) flows through Meta's servers. This is governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your Data if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or a government agency).

7. Data Retention

We retain your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy.

  • Active Accounts: Data is retained while your subscription is active.
  • Logs: Server logs and error reports are retained for 30 days for debugging purposes.
  • Meta Data: We cache Meta data (like Page Insights) for a limited time to improve dashboard performance. If you disconnect a page, this data is removed from our live database.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you have rights under GDPR (Europe), CCPA (California), or local laws:

  • Right to Access: Request copies of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data (see Section 9).
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Object to how we process your data.

9. Data Deletion Instructions

In compliance with Facebook Platform Policy, you can request the deletion of your data stored on our servers at any time.

Method 1: Automated Removal

Go to your dashboard > Settings > Profile > Click "Delete Account". This will permanently wipe your data from Supabase and disconnect all Meta tokens.

Method 2: Facebook Settings

If you remove "My Zini ERP" from your Facebook Apps and Websites settings, Facebook sends us a data deletion request. We verify this request and automatically delete associated data.

Method 3: Manual Request

Email our Data Protection Officer at contact@digitalerp.shop with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will process this within 7 business days.
