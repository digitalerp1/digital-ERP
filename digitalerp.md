## Description
Digital ERP offers a complete and free school management software to digitize your institution. Manage attendance, fees, exams, results, and staff with our all-in-one ERP solution.

* Free, All-in-One School Management Software
* Digitize your school, coaching, or business for free

  * Online fee payment
  * Exams & Results (admit card generator, online results)

  * Finance management for schools
