1. Introduction and Scope

Thank you for choosing My Zini ERP ("Company," "we," "us," or "our"), accessible via https://digitalerp.shop. We provide comprehensive Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) solutions and WhatsApp Business Automation tools (collectively, the "Services").

We recognize that privacy is a fundamental right. This Privacy Policy is a legal document that clearly outlines how we collect, process, store, and share your personal and business data. It applies to all users, visitors, and business entities accessing our platform.

By utilizing our Services, particularly our Embedded Signup feature for WhatsApp Business, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with these terms, please discontinue the use of our Services immediately.

2. Categories of Information We Collect

To provide a seamless ERP and automation experience, we collect data in the following categories:

A. Information You Provide Directly

When you register, purchase a subscription, or contact support, you may provide:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, email address, password, and recovery details.
  • Business Profile: Company name, business address, tax identification numbers (GST/VAT), and industry type.
  • Financial Information: Billing address and payment method details (Note: Sensitive card data is processed by secure third-party gateways like Razorpay/Stripe; we do not store raw credit card numbers).
  • Customer Data: Data you input into the ERP regarding your own clients, inventory, and sales. In this context, we act as a Data Processor, and you are the Data Controller.

B. Information Collected Automatically

When you interact with our platform, our servers automatically record:

  • Device Attributes: IP address, browser type, operating system version, and device model.
  • Usage Logs: Login times, pages visited, features used, and clickstream data.
  • Error Reporting: Crash logs and system performance data to help us debug issues.

3. WhatsApp Business API & Meta Platforms Integration

Crucial for Facebook Verification: My Zini ERP acts as a "Tech Provider" facilitating your access to the WhatsApp Business API provided by Meta Platforms, Inc.

3.1. Embedded Signup Data

When you use the "Log in with Facebook" or "Embedded Signup" flow on our dashboard:

  • You are interacting directly with Meta's infrastructure.
  • We receive an Access Token (System User Token) to manage your WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) on your behalf.
  • We collect your WABA ID and Phone Number ID to configure webhooks and send messages.

3.2. Message Data Processing

To enable automation features (chatbots, bulk messaging):

  • Message Content: We process the content of messages you send and receive to display them in your dashboard and trigger automation logic.
  • Metadata: We store delivery status (Sent, Delivered, Read), timestamps, and error codes returned by Meta.
  • Phone Numbers: We process the phone numbers of your end-users to transmit messages via the API.

Note: All WhatsApp data processing complies with the WhatsApp Business Policy. We do not use your message data for our own marketing purposes.

4. How We Process and Use Your Information

We process your data lawfully, fairly, and transparently for the following specific purposes:

  • Service Provisioning: To operate the ERP system, manage inventory databases, and execute WhatsApp marketing campaigns as requested by you.
  • Authentication & Security: To verify your identity, prevent unauthorized access, and protect your account from fraud.
  • Communication: To send you invoices, system updates, security alerts, and administrative messages.
  • Platform Improvement: To analyze usage trends (anonymously) to improve user interface and system performance.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and requests from law enforcement agencies.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell your personal data. However, we may share information in the following strictly defined circumstances:

5.1. Meta Platforms (Facebook/WhatsApp)

To function as a WhatsApp Automation Tool, specific data (phone numbers, template content, WABA IDs) is inevitably shared with Meta Platforms, Inc. This is a requirement for the API to function. Meta's use of this data is governed by their own Data Policy.

5.2. Service Providers (Sub-processors)

We engage trusted third-party vendors to assist in operating our business. These vendors have limited access to your data solely to perform these tasks on our behalf:

  • Cloud Hosting: PCloud Hosting & Database: Providers like Vercel, Supabase, and Google Cloud for server infrastructure and database management./li>
  • Payment Processors: To handle secure billing transactions.
  • Email Services: To deliver OTPs and notifications.

5.3. Business Transfers

If My Zini ERP is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your personal data may be transferred. We will provide notice before your Personal Data is transferred and becomes subject to a different Privacy Policy.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies (small text files stored on your device) to enhance your experience. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

  • Essential Cookies: Required for login sessions and security features. You cannot opt-out of these.
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your language preferences and dashboard settings.
  • Analytical Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with the website (e.g., Google Analytics). These are aggregated and anonymized.

7. Information Security Protocol

We implement robust technical and organizational measures ("TOMs") to protect your data:

  • Encryption: Data in transit is protected via SSL/TLS (HTTPS). Sensitive data at rest is encrypted using industry-standard algorithms (AES-256).
  • Access Control: Internal access to your data is restricted to employees who have a specific business need (e.g., customer support) and are bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Vulnerability Management: We regularly scan our systems for security vulnerabilities and patch them promptly.

Disclaimer: While we strive for absolute security, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute immunity from cyber-attacks.

8. Data Retention Policy

We retain your personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy:

  • Active Accounts: We retain data as long as your account is active to provide services.
  • Deleted Accounts: Upon account termination, we may retain certain data for up to 90 days for backup recovery purposes, after which it is permanently deleted.
  • Legal Obligations: We may retain billing and transaction records for a longer period (typically 5-7 years) to comply with tax laws and accounting standards.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR, CCPA, etc.), you have specific rights regarding your data:

Right to Access

You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Right to Rectification

You can ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.

Right to Deletion

You can request the permanent deletion of your account and data ("Right to be Forgotten").

Right to Portability

You can request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

Notice for California Residents (CCPA)

We do not "sell" personal information as defined by the CCPA. California residents have the right to request disclosure of the categories of personal information we have collected and shared.

10. Information Regarding Minors

Our Services are strictly intended for business use by individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with Personal Data, please contact us immediately. We will take steps to remove such information from our servers.
